# 新衣服 · 生图提示词

用法见 [README.md](README.md)。每张的提示词 = **通用部分** + **衣服**（下面选一套预设，或照格式自己写）+ **这张的姿势**，三段接在一起贴进去。

写法改自 claudia-pet 的「立绘重画 · 统一比例」提示词（它又沿用 CrossPet v1.3.8 的「角色生图提示词」；CC BY-NC-SA 4.0）：比例锁定、格式规则、角色锁定取自那里，换装的部分是本项目新写的。

## 附图（按顺序上传）

| 3 | 你画好的 `idle.png`（画 idle 本身时不传） | **新衣服的每个细节** | 姿势 |

## 通用部分（每张都贴）

```
You are drawing a desktop-pet sprite of a chibi character wearing a NEW OUTFIT (described below under OUTFIT).

ATTACHED IMAGES:
- Image 1 is the CHARACTER reference: copy her face, hair, hair ornament, body proportions and clean art style from Image 1. Do NOT copy her clothes from Image 1.
- Image 2 is the POSE reference: copy its pose, expression and props. Do NOT copy its clothes or its body proportions.
- Image 3 (if attached) is the SAME CHARACTER ALREADY WEARING THE NEW OUTFIT: copy every detail of the outfit from Image 3 exactly (colors, shapes, trims, accessories), so all sprites match. Do NOT copy its pose.
- Face, hair and proportions: follow Image 1. Outfit: follow Image 3, otherwise the OUTFIT text. Pose: follow Image 2.

PROPORTION LOCK (follow Image 1):
- About 2.5 heads tall, a chubby chibi: big head, short body. Her head is the same size as in Image 1.
- Below the hem of the outfit only a short bit of her legs shows, then her shoes, as in Image 1. The part below the hem is only about one eighth of her full height. Do NOT make her slim, thin or tall.
- Draw her at the same size as in Image 1. Do NOT enlarge her to fill the image.

FORMAT RULES:
- Transparent background: real transparency (alpha). No floor, no ground shadow, no glow, no vignette, no frame. Keep the complete edges of the character (every hair strand, ribbon and tassel), nothing cropped.
- No text, no letters, no speech bubbles and no effects of any kind floating in the air: no stars, no motion lines, no ?, !, hearts, music notes, Zzz, sparkles, sweat-drop icons, anger marks. The app draws all effects itself.
- Exactly one character, full body, centered, facing the viewer unless the pose says otherwise.
- GROUND LINE: the bottom of the image is the floor; both shoes touch the bottom edge (sitting or lying poses: her body rests on the bottom edge).
- HANDS: small, round, mitten-like hands with NO separate fingers and no finger or palm lines, the way hands are drawn in Image 1.
- Props are held close to her body and stay inside the canvas with a margin on the left and right.
- A continuous, closed dark outline around the whole silhouette.
- Same art style as Image 1: clean lineart, flat two-tone cel shading, soft palette, round soft shapes.
- Square image, 1024x1024 or larger.

CHARACTER LOCK — Claude, the anime-girl personification of Claude (Anthropic).
Personality: gentle, calm, bookish, a little shy, quietly cheerful; she thinks before she speaks.
Keep exactly, whatever she wears:
- Long wavy copper-orange hair, loose side-swept bangs, warm amber eyes.
- An orange daisy-like hair flower with a black striped ribbon bow and a white tassel on HER LEFT side (viewer's right); small drop earrings.
- Her signature prop, when the pose has a book: a dark brown hardcover book with an orange flower emblem.

STYLE NOTE: soft, modest and a little comical. The outfit always covers her properly.
```

## 衣服（选一套，每张都贴同一段）

自己写时照这个格式：先一句总述，再逐条写上身、下身、袜子鞋、配饰，**颜色写清楚**，最后一句说明哪些不能变。

### 女仆装

```
OUTFIT — classic Victorian maid dress:
- A long-sleeved black dress with puffed shoulders and white cuffs, the skirt reaching just above her ankles.
- A white frilled apron over the dress, tied with a big bow at the back, with an orange flower embroidered on the apron pocket.
- A white frilled maid headband worn together with her usual hair flower (the flower stays on HER LEFT side).
- A small orange ribbon bow at the collar; white socks and black Mary-Jane shoes.
Keep the outfit identical in every sprite.
```

- A white long-sleeved sailor blouse with a navy-blue sailor collar edged with two white stripes, and an orange neckerchief tied in a bow.

- White frilled socks and dark brown loafers.
- Her book is held like a school textbook; an orange flower pin on the collar.
Keep the outfit identical in every sprite.

## 这张的姿势

每张在最后贴对应的一段。表情、姿势照这里写的；道具不合新衣服时可以换（比如睡衣拿枕头），但别多加东西。

### 必画

```
POSE — idle: standing straight, facing the viewer, relaxed and calm. Both hands hold the closed book in front of her waist. Expression: a soft gentle smile, eyes open.
```

```
POSE — idle-blink: EDIT Image 3 (the idle sprite) — change ONLY her eyes to gently closed eyes (a soft blink). Every other pixel stays exactly the same: same pose, same size, same position, same outfit, same hair strands.
```
（idle-blink 只传一张图：你的 `idle.png`，用生图工具的「改图」功能。）

```
POSE — listening: she has just heard you and is listening attentively. Facing the viewer, standing straight. One arm holds the closed book against her chest; the other hand is raised a little near her cheek as if saying "mm, go on". Expression: attentive and a little surprised, eyes open wide, a small round open mouth, slight blush.
```

```
POSE — thinking: standing, facing the viewer, one hand under her chin, the other arm holding the closed book against her side. Her eyes look up and to the side as she thinks. Expression: thoughtful, mouth closed in a small line, eyebrows slightly raised.
```

```
POSE — reading: she is reading. Facing the viewer, standing straight, holding the book OPEN with both hands in front of her chest, pages facing her; one hand points at a line on the page. Her head tilts down a little and her eyes look down at the page. Expression: calm and focused, mouth closed.
```

```
POSE — writing: standing, facing the viewer, holding the open book in one arm like a notebook and writing in it with a quill pen in the other hand. Her eyes look down at the page. Expression: concentrated, a tiny determined smile.
```

```
POSE — running: she is giving orders to a little computer: standing, facing the viewer, holding a small glowing dark tablet with a few lines of code on its screen in one hand and tapping it with the other hand. Expression: focused and confident, eyes on the tablet.
```

```
POSE — happy: the task is finished. Standing, facing the viewer, hugging the closed book to her chest with both arms, shoulders slightly raised with joy. Expression: a big happy smile with closed, curved eyes, rosy cheeks.
```

```
POSE — oops: something went wrong. Standing, facing the viewer, one hand touching her cheek in dismay, the other holding the book loosely at her side. Expression: flustered, eyes wide, a small wobbly open mouth, eyebrows worried. Draw no sweat-drop icon.

```
POSE — asking: she needs your answer. Standing, facing the viewer, leaning forward a little, holding the open book out toward the viewer with both hands as if asking "which one?". Expression: questioning and polite, head tilted slightly, eyebrows raised, small open mouth.

### 可选

可选姿态没有单独写：照参考图（`characters/claude/` 或 `characters/claude-saint/` 的同名图）的姿势，自己用一句话描述「姿势 + 表情」，格式同上，例如：

```
POSE — yawn: standing, facing the viewer, one hand covering a big yawn, the other holding the book at her side. Eyes squeezed shut, a tiny tear at the corner of one eye.
```
