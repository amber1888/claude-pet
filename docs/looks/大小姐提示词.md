# 三位大小姐 · 生图提示词

用 AI 生图把三位大小姐画成和默认形象一样精致的立绘。流程和 [README.md](README.md) 一样，只是提示词换成这份：

**每张的提示词 = 通用部分（大小姐版）+ 这位大小姐的「人物 + 衣服」+ 这张的姿势**，三段接在一起贴进去。

三位各用自己的发色和发型（金色螺旋卷 / 黑长直 / 银发），脸型、身体比例、画风照默认形象，头上都别着 claudia 的小橙花。
想让她们都是 claudia 的橙发，把「人物」里写头发的那一行换成 `Long wavy copper-orange hair…`（见 [提示词.md](提示词.md) 的 CHARACTER LOCK）就行。

## 附图（按顺序上传）

| 顺序 | 文件 | 管什么 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `characters/claude/idle.webp` | 画风、脸型、身体比例（**不管**头发和衣服） |
| 2 | 这个姿态的参考图（见文末的表） | 大致的姿势；和姿势文字对不上时以文字为准 |
| 3 | 你画好的这位的 `idle.png`（画 idle 本身时不传） | 头发、眼睛、衣服、配饰的每个细节，保证每张一致 |

**先画 idle**，满意了再画别的；螺旋卷、礼帽、编发这些容易走样，不像就重画，别将就。
`idle-blink` 用生图工具的「改图」：只传你的 `idle.png`，贴下面那句。

## 通用部分（大小姐版，每张都贴）

```
You are drawing a desktop-pet sprite of a chibi anime girl, a little noble young lady (ojou-sama), described below under CHARACTER and OUTFIT.

ATTACHED IMAGES:
- Image 1 is the STYLE and PROPORTION reference: copy its art style, its face shape and its body proportions. Do NOT copy its hair color, hairstyle, hair ornaments or clothes; those come from CHARACTER and OUTFIT.
- Image 2 is the POSE reference: copy its general pose and body angle only. Do NOT copy its clothes, hair or proportions. If Image 2 and the POSE text disagree, follow the POSE text.
- Image 3 (if attached) is the SAME CHARACTER ALREADY DRAWN: copy her hair, eyes, outfit and accessories from Image 3 exactly (colors, shapes, trims, ribbons), so all sprites match. Do NOT copy its pose.

PROPORTION LOCK (follow Image 1):
- About 2.5 heads tall, a chubby chibi: big head, short body. Her head is the same size as in Image 1.
- Below the hem of the skirt only a short bit of her legs shows, then her shoes. That part is only about one eighth of her full height. Do NOT make her slim, thin or tall.
- Draw her at the same size as in Image 1. Do NOT enlarge her to fill the image.

FORMAT RULES:
- Transparent background: real transparency (alpha). No floor, no ground shadow, no glow, no vignette, no frame. Keep the complete edges of the character (every hair strand, curl, ribbon and lace), nothing cropped.
- No text, no letters, no speech bubbles and no effects of any kind floating in the air: no stars, no motion lines, no ?, !, hearts, music notes, Zzz, sparkles, sweat-drop icons, anger marks. The app draws all effects itself.
- Exactly one character, full body, centered, facing the viewer unless the pose says otherwise.
- GROUND LINE: the bottom of the image is the floor; both shoes touch the bottom edge (sitting poses: her skirt rests on the bottom edge).
- HANDS: small, round, mitten-like hands with NO separate fingers and no finger or palm lines, the way hands are drawn in Image 1.
- Props are held close to her body and stay inside the canvas with a margin on the left and right.
- A continuous, closed dark outline around the whole silhouette.
- Same art style as Image 1: soft anime illustration, clean delicate lineart, soft cel shading, glossy hair with many fine strands, big detailed sparkling eyes, warm soft palette.
- Square image, 1024x1024 or larger.

STYLE NOTE: cute, elegant and a little comical. The outfit always covers her properly.
```

## 人物 + 衣服（选一位，这位的每张都贴同一段）

### 金卷大小姐（ojou-gold）

```
CHARACTER — "Golden-Curl Ojou-sama": a proud, cheerful rich young lady who laughs "Ohohoho!"; generous and a little vain.
Keep exactly in every sprite:
- Bright golden-blonde hair with TWO BIG VERTICAL DRILL CURLS (long tight spiral ringlets) hanging on both sides of her head down to her waist; soft side-swept bangs; one small cowlick (ahoge) on top of her head.
- Big sapphire-blue eyes with sparkling highlights, rosy cheeks.
- Two large crimson ribbon bows where the drill curls start, one on each side of her head; a small orange daisy-like flower pinned above the ribbon on HER LEFT side (viewer's right).
- Signature prop: a closed golden folding fan (sensu) with a tiny gold tassel. When a pose says "open lace fan", it is a pink-and-white lace folding fan with gold ribs.

OUTFIT — rose-pink ball-gown lolita dress:
- Pink puffed short sleeves with white lace trim, then fitted pink sleeves to the wrists with white frilled cuffs.
- A round white lace collar with a crimson ribbon bow at the throat and a round gold brooch in the bow's center.
- A high waist with a crimson sash.
- A very full, bell-shaped two-tier pink skirt reaching her knees: the upper tier edged with white lace and decorated with three small crimson bows; layered white petticoat frills peek out under the hem.
- White tights and glossy crimson Mary-Jane shoes.
Keep the outfit identical in every sprite.
```

## 画好以后

每位一个文件夹，文件名就是姿态名（`idle.png`、`thinking.png`……，同一姿态多画一张叫 `happy-2.png`），放进仓库的
`looks/ojou-gold-art/`、`looks/ojou-noir-art/`、`looks/ojou-lilac-art/`，再跑 `./scripts/build.sh`：
`scripts/art-looks.sh` 会对齐、缩放、配上已经写好的台词（`looks/<id>/character.json`），没画的姿态拿相近的一张顶上。

现在每位画了 10 个必画的姿态；照上面的提示词补画 proud、surprised、sleeping 这些，放进同一个文件夹重新构建就会换上。
